# Title
Personal secretary

# Description
A auto-reminder integrated personal knowledge management system. Users can research on a topic, and as a result, it might require a reminder to remind users that they should do something at a certain time, so they can easier to handle a long-term task management.

# Use Cases

### 1. Reminder

- 1. Users did a research on Australia LHC policy and had been aware of that they should reactivate their insurance policy within 30 days after the landing of returning to Australia, after they paused their policy when they left Australia, in order to avoid unnecessary LHC penalty.
- 2. Users asked a reminder at a certain time.
- 3. UI/server received this request, and set up a reminder that ensure users will receive a notification once the reactivation is about to be required. This reminder could be one week in advance, and another reminder when it closes to the deadline.
